Our Story

From the day we met to the day we said “yes”, these are the moments that brought us here.

01

The beginning

How We Met

In 2013, Nick was studying in Birmingham and Belinda in Sheffield when, during their second year at university, a birthday party brought them together. Nick happened to live with one of Belinda’s old school friends, who invited her along. They exchanged numbers that evening and spent the following weeks talking constantly over Skype.

For the next two years, they took turns making the coach journey every other weekend between Birmingham and Sheffield. Every reunion at the station brought the same rush of butterflies.

02

Along the way

Our Adventures

Their first holiday abroad together was Belgium, where Belinda’s fear of heights faced an early test in the glass lift to the top of the Atomium. Next came Paphos, their first beach holiday. It was the beginning of a lasting love for Cyprus, now a favourite destination for its food and boat trips.

After graduating, they began a new adventure closer to home when they moved into their first flat together in Bracknell. Since then, they have climbed Snowdon and travelled to Tenerife and Santa Cruz, Madeira, Ayia Napa, Paris, Berlin, Gran Canaria, Thailand, Turkey, Amsterdam and Cannes. There are still plenty more places they would love to explore together.

03

The easiest yes

The Proposal

On a 15-day trip to Thailand, their most unforgettable adventure yet, Nick carried a ring hidden in his backpack while he waited for the right moment. It travelled with them to the Full Moon Party, Koh Samui, the floating bungalows of Khao Sok, an elephant sanctuary, the floating and railway markets, and Ao Nang.

Near the end of the holiday, they returned to Bangkok. While walking through the crowded city, they found a peaceful garden tucked away in a business park. The noise disappeared and there was not another person around. Beside a large pond filled with hundreds of koi, they walked out along a pier. That was the moment Nick took the ring from his backpack, got down on one knee and asked Belinda to marry him.
