Find us

Eastington Park is less than two minutes from Junction 13 of the M5, halfway between Bristol and Cheltenham.

Take the A419 towards Stroud. At the Starbucks and Shell petrol station roundabout, take the third exit, signposted Eastington.

The entrance is on your left shortly after crossing the canal, approximately 200 yards from the roundabout.

FAQ

A few helpful answers as you get ready to celebrate with us.

Your invitation & RSVP

When should I RSVP by?

Please reply by 29 January 2027. If we haven’t received your response by then, we’ll assume you’re unable to attend so that we can confirm final guest numbers with our suppliers.

Please use the RSVP section to confirm attendance and tell us about any dietary requirements or allergies. We can’t wait to celebrate with you! The household form is currently a preview using sample invitations.

Can I respond for my partner or family?

Yes — one person can reply for everyone named on the invitation. Confirm each guest’s attendance and choose a starter and main for everyone who accepts. Dietary requirements are recorded individually, with one contact email and optional song suggestion for your invitation.

Are children welcome?

As much as we would love to include all our friends’ and families’ children, we’re only able to accommodate children who are named on the invitation. Thank you for understanding.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Due to limited space, we’re unable to accommodate plus-ones unless specified on your invitation. We hope you understand and will still join us to celebrate.

What should I do if I’m unable to attend?

We’d love to celebrate with you, but understand that it may not be possible for everyone. Please let us know as soon as possible so that we can plan accordingly. You can decline in your RSVP or get in touch with us.

Getting ready for the day

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive at St Michael & All Angels Church at 12:15pm and be seated by 12:30pm at the latest, so our ceremony can start on time. If driving, park at Eastington Park first and allow time for the walk to the church.

See our order of the day for the rest of the celebrations.

Can I arrive at the church before midday?

Yes, you’re welcome to arrive at St Michael & All Angels Church up to an hour before the ceremony. If driving, please park at Eastington Park and allow time for the approximately 10-minute walk to the church.

Where should I park?

Please park at Eastington Park and leave your car there for the celebrations. Parking at the church is restricted. Eastington Park is approximately a 10-minute walk from the church, so please leave enough time to arrive and be seated.

If you have mobility or access needs, please speak to us beforehand so we can discuss the arrangements with you.

Is there a dress code or colour theme?

There’s no set colour theme, but for a little inspiration, think elegant summer garden party. We’d love you to dress up and wear something that makes you feel comfortable and confident — a summer dress, a suit or something in between.

Please avoid jeans, sportswear, white and ivory. We look forward to seeing you all looking fabulous!

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

Eastington Park’s communal areas are on the ground floor. Please get in touch about your individual requirements so we can discuss access at the venue and church, and travel between the two.

Photos & celebrations

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We’ve chosen an unplugged ceremony so everyone can be fully present for this special moment. Please put phones and cameras away during the ceremony.

Our professional photographer and content creator will capture the moments for us, and we’d hate for anyone to accidentally obstruct their view. We’ll be delighted to share the photos afterwards. Once the ceremony is over, please snap away and enjoy capturing the celebrations!

Can I share photos on social media?

We’d love you to share the celebrations! Please wait to post any photos of the bride and groom until we’ve shared our first wedding photos ourselves. Thank you for understanding.

What time does the wedding end?

Our DJ finishes at midnight, but we’ll continue celebrating in the bar and adjoining rooms until carriages at 1am on Thursday, 10 June. There may even be a little karaoke for those who fancy taking to the mic!

A little more planning

Where can I stay?

Our Places to Stay section includes local hotels, an interactive map and availability links. Please check your dates, prices and room requirements directly with your chosen hotel or booking provider.

How do I tell you about allergies or dietary requirements?

Please add any allergies or dietary requirements to the relevant guest’s notes when replying for your household. If there’s anything you’d like to discuss, please contact us directly so we can speak with you and our suppliers.

Can I suggest a song?

Absolutely! Add a song title and artist to your household RSVP. Help us curate a playlist that will get you on the dance floor.

Should I arrange a taxi in advance?

Yes — please arrange your journey home in advance and confirm your collection time directly with your provider. Our taxi options include A&A Taxis, Stroud Taxi and Uber.

We’re here to help

Still have a question?

If you have any questions that we haven’t answered on our wedding website, please don’t hesitate to get in touch with either of us. We’re more than happy to help and want to make sure you have everything you need to enjoy our special day with us!
