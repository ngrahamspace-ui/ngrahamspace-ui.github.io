The ceremony

St Michael & All Angels Church, Eastington

Please arrive at 12:15pm for our 12:30pm ceremony. The reception follows at Eastington Park from 2pm.

Church directions

By car

Eastington Park, Spring Hill, Eastington, Gloucestershire GL10 3RY

The venue is close to Junction 13 of the M5. Follow the A419 towards Stroud, then signs for Eastington.

Venue directions

By train

Stonehouse is the closest station, around two miles from Eastington Park. The venue advises that direct trains from London Paddington take around 95 minutes. Please check the timetable for your travel date and prebook a taxi for the last part of your journey.

Plan your rail journeyVenue travel guidance

Flying in

Bristol is the closest airport. London Heathrow is another option for international arrivals. Please allow time for your onward journey to Gloucestershire and arrange a hire car, rail connection or transfer.

A few helpful answers

FAQ

If there’s anything else you’d like to know, just get in touch with us.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive at St Michael & All Angels Church at 12:15pm for the 12:30pm ceremony. Our preliminary order of the day has all the timings.

Where can I stay?

Our Places to Stay section includes Premier Inn Stroud, Holiday Inn Express Gloucester – South, Stonehouse Court Hotel and The Stroud Hotel. Choose your dates to check availability with the booking provider, or visit the hotel’s own website.

How do I RSVP and choose my meal?

Please send one RSVP per guest. If you’re attending, choose one starter and one main, and tick any wines you enjoy. We’re serving a trio of desserts, so there’s no dessert choice to make. Please reply by 29 January 2027.

How can I tell you about allergies or accessibility needs?

Please include any allergies, dietary requirements or accessibility needs in the notes on your RSVP. You can also contact us directly so we can discuss arrangements with you and the venue.

Can I request a song?

Absolutely — add the song title and artist to your RSVP. Help us curate a playlist that will get you on the dance floor!

When should I book my taxi home?

Carriages are planned for 1am on Thursday, 10 June. Please arrange your taxi in advance if you’re staying away from the venue. See our taxi options for A&A Taxis, Stroud Taxi and Uber.

Who should I contact with another question?

Just speak to us, Nick and Belinda, or email n.graham.space@gmail.com. We’ll be happy to help.
